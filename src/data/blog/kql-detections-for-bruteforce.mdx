## Detection objective

I needed a rule that could detect repeated failed authentication attempts and surface only meaningful incidents.

## Rule design approach

- Group by account and source IP
- Use a short rolling time window
- Add thresholds for failed attempts
- Include host and timestamp context for triage

## Tuning process

I iterated on thresholds after reviewing alert history.  
The focus was reducing noisy events while preserving suspicious behavior.

## What improved quality

- Excluding known benign sources
- Enriching entities (user, IP, host)
- Adding severity mapping based on volume and frequency

The final rule gave much cleaner triage and faster analyst decisions.
